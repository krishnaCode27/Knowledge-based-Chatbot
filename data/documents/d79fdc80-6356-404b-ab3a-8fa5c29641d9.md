# Information Security, Data Privacy & Access Control Policy

## 1. Governance and Regulatory Compliance
The Information Security Office (ISO) establishes and enforces institutional safeguards across all digital platforms at Aethelgard Institute of Technology, including the Knowledge AI platform. The policies herein comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for international affiliates, and ISO/IEC 27001 standards.

## 2. Data Classification Tiers
All institutional digital assets and Markdown documents are classified into four tiers:
* **Tier 1: Public:** Freely publishable data (e.g., Academic Calendar, Public Course Catalog). No encryption required at rest.
* **Tier 2: Internal / Restricted:** Information intended exclusively for students and staff (e.g., Campus Facility Guides, IT Support Policies). Requires authenticated NetID access.
* **Tier 3: Confidential:** Sensitive records (e.g., Student academic transcripts, disciplinary hearings, employee evaluations). Requires Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and AES-256 encryption.
* **Tier 4: Highly Restricted:** Highly protected identifiers (e.g., Social Security Numbers, banking credentials, raw encryption keys). Strict segregation, multi-person authorization, and immutable access logging required.

## 3. Cryptographic Standards and Transmission Security
* **Data in Transit:** All HTTP traffic must enforce **TLS 1.3**; TLS 1.1 and 1.0 are disabled at the ingress load balancers. Plain HTTP connections on port 80 are permanently redirected to HTTPS on port 443 with HSTS enabled (`max-age=31536000`).
* **Data at Rest:** All persistent volumes, vector databases (Qdrant), relational databases, and disk backups must be encrypted using **AES-256 (CBC or GCM mode)** with keys managed via AWS KMS or HashiCorp Vault.
* **Cryptographic Hash Functions:** Only **SHA-256**, **SHA-512**, or **bcrypt/Argon2id** (for password hashing with cost factor >= 12) are permitted. MD5 and SHA-1 are strictly prohibited.

## 5. Security Incident Response Protocol
In the event of a suspected data breach or unauthorized vector corpus exfiltration:
1. **Phase 1: Detection & Reporting (Within 30 Minutes):** Any user or engineer observing anomalous query spikes or unauthorized API token usage must email `security-incident@aethelgard.edu` or page the ISO emergency duty officer at extension x9911.
2. **Phase 2: Immediate Containment (Within 1 Hour):** Network engineering revokes compromised API tokens, invalidates active Redis sessions, and activates the WAF geo-blocking rules.
3. **Phase 3: Forensic Eradication & Remediation (Within 24 Hours):** Audit logs are exported to write-once-read-many (WORM) storage. Root-cause patches are tested and deployed.
4. **Phase 4: Regulatory Notification (Within 72 Hours):** If Confidential (Tier 3) or Highly Restricted (Tier 4) personal information is compromised, formal notices to affected parties and regulatory commissioners are delivered in accordance with statutory requirements.
