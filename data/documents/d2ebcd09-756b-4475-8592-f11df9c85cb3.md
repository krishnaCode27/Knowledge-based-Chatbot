# Academic Calendar & Key Institutional Milestones (Academic Year 2026–2027)

## 1. Fall Semester 2026
* **August 17, 2026 (Monday):** Faculty reporting date and departmental orientation.
* **August 24, 2026 (Monday):** Fall semester instructional classes commence.
* **September 4, 2026 (Friday):** Last day for course registration changes (Add/Drop Deadline at 17:00 EST).
* **September 7, 2026 (Monday):** Labor Day — Institute closed, no classes held.
* **October 12 – 16, 2026 (Monday – Friday):** Mid-Semester Examination Week.
* **November 6, 2026 (Friday):** Final day to withdraw from courses with a grade of `W` (Withdrawal Deadline).
* **November 25 – 27, 2026 (Wednesday – Friday):** Thanksgiving Recess — Campus open, classes suspended.
* **December 4, 2026 (Friday):** Final day of regular Fall semester instruction.
* **December 7 – 11, 2026 (Monday – Friday):** Fall Semester Final Examinations.
* **December 16, 2026 (Wednesday):** Final grade submission deadline for faculty (23:59 EST).
* **December 18, 2026 – January 8, 2027:** Winter Intersession / Campus Administrative Shutdown.

## 2. Spring Semester 2027
* **January 11, 2027 (Monday):** Spring semester instructional classes commence.
* **January 18, 2027 (Monday):** Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Institute closed.
* **January 22, 2027 (Friday):** Add/Drop Deadline for Spring semester courses (17:00 EST).
* **March 1 – 5, 2027 (Monday – Friday):** Mid-Semester Examination Week.
* **March 15 – 19, 2027 (Monday – Friday):** Spring Break Recess — Classes suspended.
* **April 2, 2027 (Friday):** Final course withdrawal deadline (`W` grade).
* **April 30, 2027 (Friday):** Final day of Spring semester instruction.
* **May 3 – 7, 2027 (Monday – Friday):** Spring Semester Final Examinations.
* **May 12, 2027 (Wednesday):** Final grade submission deadline for faculty (23:59 EST).
* **May 16, 2027 (Sunday):** 74th Annual Commencement & Graduation Exercises.

## 3. Summer Term 2027
* **May 24, 2027 (Monday):** Summer Session I (6-week intensive) begins.
* **June 18, 2027 (Friday):** Juneteenth Holiday observance — Institute closed.
* **July 2, 2027 (Friday):** Summer Session I concludes; final exams administered.
* **July 5, 2027 (Monday):** Independence Day Holiday observance — Institute closed.
* **July 6, 2027 (Tuesday):** Summer Session II (6-week intensive) begins.
* **August 13, 2027 (Friday):** Summer Session II concludes and Summer Term terminates.
