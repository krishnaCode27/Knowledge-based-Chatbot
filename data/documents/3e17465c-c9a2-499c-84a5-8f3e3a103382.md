# Human Resources — Faculty & Staff Employee Handbook

## 1. Employment Classifications
Employees at Aethelgard Institute of Technology are categorized into three distinct operational classifications:
1. **Full-Time Regular Faculty & Staff:** Contracted for 37.5 to 40 hours per week; entitled to comprehensive medical, retirement, and tuition benefits.
2. **Part-Time Staff:** Contracted for fewer than 29 hours per week; statutory benefits apply; ineligible for tuition remission.
3. **Adjunct Lecturers / Visiting Scholars:** Term-contracted per instructional credit hour.

## 2. Standard Working Hours and Flexible Arrangements
* **Standard Workweek:** Monday through Friday, 08:30 to 17:00 EST, including a mandatory 60-minute unpaid meal break.
* **Core Institutional Hours:** 10:00 to 15:00 EST. All full-time administrative staff must be available for meetings and collaborative duties during this window.
* **Telecommuting / Remote Work Policy:**
  * Full-time staff members who have completed their initial **90-day probationary period** may request up to **2 days per week** of remote work.
  * Remote work schedules require written approval from the Department Head and the Vice President of Human Resources.
  * Faculty members manage instructional and office hours according to their approved departmental syllabus.

## 3. Leave Entitlements and Accrual
Leave must be requested via the Workday HR portal at least **10 business days** in advance for scheduled vacations.

### Annual Leave Allocations (Full-Time Staff)
| Employment Tenure | Paid Vacation Days / Year | Sick Leave Accrual | Personal Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Years 1 – 3 | 15 days (accrued at 1.25 d/mo) | 12 days per year (1.0 d/mo) | 3 days per calendar year |
| Years 4 – 7 | 20 days (accrued at 1.67 d/mo) | 12 days per year (1.0 d/mo) | 3 days per calendar year |
| 8+ Years | 25 days (accrued at 2.08 d/mo) | 12 days per year (1.0 d/mo) | 3 days per calendar year |

* **Leave Rollover:** A maximum of **5 accrued vacation days** may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year. Unused rollover days expire on March 31.
* **Bereavement Leave:** Up to **5 consecutive business days** for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling); up to **3 days** for extended family.

## 4. Professional Development and Tuition Remission
* **Tuition Assistance:** Full-time employees after one year of continuous service are eligible for a **100% tuition remission waiver** for up to two degree-credit courses per semester at AIT.
* **Professional Conference Allowance:** Full-time staff and tenure-track faculty are allocated up to **$2,200 annually** for academic conference travel, registration, and professional society memberships, subject to dean approval.

## 5. Performance Reviews and Grievance Procedure
* **Annual Evaluations:** Formal performance assessments occur each year between May 15 and June 30.
* **Grievance Resolution:** Informal mediation must first be sought through the HR Employee Relations Officer. If unresolved within **14 business days**, a formal grievance may be filed before the Institutional Staff Grievance Committee.
