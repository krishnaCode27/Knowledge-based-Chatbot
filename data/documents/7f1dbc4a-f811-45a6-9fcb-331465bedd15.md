# Campus Facilities, Laboratories & Equipment Guide

## 1. Facility Overview
Aethelgard Institute of Technology spans six primary physical structures:
1. **Founder's Hall (Building A):** Executive administration, admissions, and financial aid.
2. **Turing Engineering Center (Building T):** Computing labs, server rooms, ITS helpdesk, robotics hangar.
3. **Curie Science Pavilion (Building C):** Chemistry wet labs, molecular biology cleanrooms, physics research wings.
4. **Augustus Vance Memorial Library (Building L):** Archives, study carrels, digital commons.
5. **Kepler Lecture Complex (Building K):** Auditorium 101, Auditorium 102, tiered seminar rooms.
6. **Da Vinci Creative Arts Center (Building D):** Design studios, fabrication lab (FabLab), digital media suites.

## 2. Specialized Laboratories and Safety Protocols
* **Robotics & Mechatronics Bay (Room T-210):**
  * Houses six industrial 6-axis robotic arms and rapid prototyping CNC mills.
  * Prerequisites: Completion of Safety Module SAFE-101 and wearing ANSI Z87.1 certified safety glasses.
  * Supervision: Requires a designated lab proctor present for all undergraduate operations.
* **Cleanroom Fabrication Suite (Room C-015):**
  * ISO Class 5 cleanroom environment for semiconductor and microfluidics research.
  * Entry requires full Tyvek gowning procedure, nitrile gloves, and air-shower cycling for 30 seconds.
  * Maximum simultaneous occupancy: **12 researchers**.

## 3. Reservation Policies and Booking Rules
Classrooms, tiered lecture halls, and laboratory workstations must be reserved using the central portal (`facilities.aethelgard.edu`).

### Reservation Rules by Space Category
| Space Category | Advance Reservation Window | Maximum Duration Per Booking | Approval Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standard Study Rooms (Library) | Up to 7 days in advance | 2 hours per group per day | Instant automated booking |
| Seminar Rooms (K-201 to K-208) | Up to 14 days in advance | 4 consecutive hours | Departmental Admin approval |
| Kepler Auditorium 101 (500 seats)| Up to 90 days in advance | Full-day event block | Facilities Director sign-off |
| FabLab 3D Printing Station | Up to 3 days in advance | 3 hours machine time | Lab technician check-in |

### Cancellation & No-Show Penalties
* Cancellations must be logged at least **4 hours prior** to the scheduled reservation.
* Failing to check in within **15 minutes** of the start time forfeits the reservation to walk-in users.
* Accumulating **three unexcused no-shows** in a single semester suspends facility reservation privileges for **30 calendar days**.

## 4. Equipment Lending Desk
Located at Turing Building Room T-108 (adjacent to the hardware storage bay).
* **Available Equipment:** High-end mirrorless camera kits, portable oscilloscopes, field audio recorders, and VR headsets (Meta Quest Pro / HTC Vive).
* **Lending Period:** Maximum **48 hours** for standard course projects; up to **7 days** with written thesis advisor endorsement.
* **Late Fee:** **$15.00 per day** overdue fee; damage or loss billed at manufacturer retail value plus 10% restocking surcharge.
