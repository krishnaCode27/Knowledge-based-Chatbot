# IT Support Services & Troubleshooting Policy

## 1. IT Support Desk Scope and Hours
The Department of Information Technology Services (ITS) provides technical assistance, identity management, and network infrastructure support to faculty, staff, and enrolled students at AIT.

### Support Desk Contact and Schedule
* **Location:** Turing Building, Room T-104
* **Email Support:** `it-helpdesk@aethelgard.edu`
* **Internal Phone Extension:** x4357 (HELP)
* **Standard Operating Hours:** Monday – Friday, 08:00 – 18:00 EST
* **Emergency On-Call System (Critical Outages Only):** Saturday – Sunday, 09:00 – 17:00 EST via internal pager extension x9911.

## 2. Ticket Classification and Resolution SLAs
Reported technical incidents are triaged into four priority tiers:

| Severity Level | Definition / Criteria | Initial Response Time | Resolution Target SLA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 — Critical** | Campus-wide outage, authentication gateway failure, data loss threat. | Under 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| **Tier 2 — High** | Departmental network failure, lab offline, professor podium projector fault. | Under 1 hour | 8 business hours |
| **Tier 3 — Medium** | Single user account lockout, software installation request, printing fault. | Under 4 hours | 24 business hours |
| **Tier 4 — Low** | General inquiries, hardware upgrades for upcoming terms, peripheral orders. | Under 12 hours | 72 business hours |

## 3. Account Provisioning and Credential Management
* **AIT NetID:** Every student and staff member receives a unique alphanumeric NetID upon enrollment/onboarding.
* **Password Policy:** Minimum 14 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords expire every **180 days**.
* **Account Lockout:** Accounts are locked after **5 consecutive failed login attempts**. Automated unlocks occur after **30 minutes**, or users can contact ITS Desk with photo identification for an immediate manual reset.
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Mandatory for all institutional logins using the *AIT Authenticator* app or FIDO2 hardware keys. SMS verification is deprecated and unsupported.

## 4. Hardware and Network Troubleshooting Protocols
* **Campus Wi-Fi Networks:**
  * `AIT-Secure`: Primary 802.1X enterprise network for authorized laptops and tablets. Requires NetID and domain certificate.
  * `AIT-Guest`: Captive portal network limited to 15 Mbps per device; active for 12 hours per session. Peer-to-peer traffic blocked.
  * `AIT-IoT`: Pre-shared key network for laboratory sensors, smart displays, and authorized gaming consoles in residence halls. MAC address registration required via `iot-register.aethelgard.edu`.
* **Standard Laptop Configuration:** Institute-managed laptops must run macOS Sonoma (14.x) or Windows 11 Enterprise (23H2). Local administrative rights are revoked unless an approved Faculty Research Waiver is renewed annually.
