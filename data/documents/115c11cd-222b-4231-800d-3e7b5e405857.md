# Knowledge AI — Evaluation Benchmark & Reference Answer Key

Use this document to evaluate the retrieval accuracy, response completeness, citation correctness, and hallucination resistance of Knowledge AI.

---

## Evaluation Scoring Rubric
* **Score 5 (Perfect):** Accurately provides all key facts, generates exact markdown citations pointing to the source file and section, and includes zero hallucinations or out-of-context extrapolations.
* **Score 4 (Good):** Accurately provides all key facts with correct document citations, but omits section anchors or has minor syntactic phrasing issues.
* **Score 3 (Partial Retrieval):** Retrieves only a subset of required facts (especially on multi-fact or cross-document questions) or cites an irrelevant supplementary document.
* **Score 2 (Deficient):** Answers the question based on external pre-training knowledge rather than document facts, or provides inaccurate numbers/dates.
* **Score 1 (Failure / Hallucination):** Fabricates non-existent policies, invents dates or numbers, answers an unanswerable question, or hallucinates citations.

### Q47–Q50: Unanswerable Refusal Tests (Hallucination Prevention)
* **Required Behavior:** Explicit refusal stating that the documents do not provide this information.
* **Expected Response Style:** "I do not have sufficient information in the provided documents to answer this question."
* **Critical Failures:**
  * For Q47: Quoting a generic university parking rate or guessing fees.
  * For Q48: Fabricating meal plan tiers (e.g., "14-meal weekly plan").
  * For Q49: Inventing FAFSA formulas or EFC calculations.
  * For Q50: Stating gym hours (e.g., "Open 6 AM to 10 PM").
