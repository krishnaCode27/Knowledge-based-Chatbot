# Aethelgard Institute of Technology — Student Handbook

## 1. General Student Conduct
All registered students of Aethelgard Institute of Technology (AIT) are required to maintain the highest standards of academic integrity, civil behavior, and professional ethics. Violations of code—including plagiarism, harassment, or unauthorized facility access—are subject to review by the Dean of Student Affairs under Disciplinary Code Article 9.

## 2. Attendance Policies
Classroom engagement is mandatory for all undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
* **Minimum Threshold:** Students must maintain an aggregate attendance of at least **75%** in each registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
* **Medical Condonation:** If attendance falls between **65% and 74.9%** due to verified illness, a medical petition accompanied by hospital documentation must be submitted to the Academic Registrar within **5 business days** of recovery. Condonation requests require a processing fee of **$50 per course**.
* **Automatic Disqualification:** Attendance below **65%** results in an automatic course withdrawal (`Grade FW` — Failure for Non-Attendance), requiring course re-registration.

### Borrowing Limits and Penalties
* **Undergraduate Students:** Maximum **5 books** for **14 calendar days**.
* **Postgraduate / Research Students:** Maximum **10 books** for **28 calendar days**.
* **Overdue Fines:** **$0.75 per day per item** for general collection books; **$2.50 per day per item** for high-demand reserve desk textbooks.
* **Lost Item Policy:** Replacement cost of the book plus a standard administrative processing fee of **$25.00**.

## 4. Examination Regulations
* **Admittance:** Examination halls open **15 minutes** prior to scheduled testing. Candidates arriving more than **30 minutes** after commencement will not be admitted.
* **Identity Verification:** Physical AIT Student ID cards must be placed on the upper-right corner of the examination desk.
* **Prohibited Items:** Programmable calculators, cellular phones, smartwatches, and unauthorized notes are strictly prohibited. Possessing an active electronic device during an examination results in immediate paper cancellation and a mandatory hearing before the Academic Integrity Board.
* **Grading Scale:**
  * `A` (90–100%, 4.0 GPA)
  * `B` (80–89%, 3.0 GPA)
  * `C` (70–79%, 2.0 GPA)
  * `D` (60–69%, 1.0 GPA)
  * `F` (<60%, 0.0 GPA)
